The Right Wallpaper Size for Your iPhone

iPhone wallpapers look sharpest when they match your screen's native portrait resolution. Here are the sizes for current models:

  • iPhone 16 Pro Max / 15 Pro Max: 1320×2868 / 1290×2796
  • iPhone 16 Pro / 15 Pro: 1206×2622 / 1179×2556
  • iPhone 16 Plus / 15 Plus: 1290×2796
  • iPhone 16 / 15 / 14: 1179×2556 (14: 1170×2532)
  • iPhone SE (3rd gen): 750×1334

The wallpaper maker lets you enter any custom width and height, so you can match your model exactly — or make it slightly larger, which never hurts.

Lock screen vs. home screen: iOS may crop, zoom, or shift your wallpaper slightly (for the parallax/perspective effect and lock screen layouts). Keep your text near the center and leave a comfortable margin around it so nothing gets cut off on either screen.

How to Make a Text Wallpaper for iPhone

  1. Open the maker in your browser — go to TextWallpaper.com on your iPhone (or any device).
  2. Type your text — and pick your text color, background color, and size.
  3. Set the resolution — use your iPhone's screen size from the list above.
  4. Download — the image saves to your Photos.
  5. Set it as wallpaper — open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos, choose your image, then set it for the lock screen, home screen, or both. (You can also long-press the lock screen to customize it directly.)

Ideas for Your Custom Phone Wallpaper

  • Your name — a simple name wallpaper makes your phone unmistakably yours.
  • A daily reminder — "Drink water", "Call mom", "Focus" — you'll see it dozens of times a day.
  • A favorite quote — short quotes work best on a phone screen.
  • Minimal aesthetic — a single word on a soft, muted background.
  • Black background, white text — the classic look; it also saves battery on OLED iPhones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No watermarks, no sign-up, no paid tier.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It's an online wallpaper maker that runs in your browser. Your text never leaves your device — the image is generated locally.

What resolution should I use?

Your iPhone's native resolution (see the size list above). Anything equal to or larger than your screen will look crisp.

Does it work on Android?

Yes — the same tool works on any phone. Just enter your Android phone's screen resolution and set the downloaded image as your wallpaper from your gallery.
