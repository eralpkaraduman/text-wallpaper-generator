Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

TextWallpaper.com has no accounts, no sign-ups, and collects no personal data from you. Your wallpapers are generated entirely inside your browser — the text you type is never uploaded to any server.

What happens in your browser

Everything. The text you enter, the colors you pick, and the images you create are processed and rendered locally on your device. No wallpaper content, text, or images are transmitted to, or stored on, our servers.

Analytics

The site uses privacy-respecting, anonymized usage analytics to understand which features get used — never who is using them. This includes aggregate page views and feature-usage events (for example, how often the download button is clicked). We do not build profiles of individual visitors, and we do not sell or share analytics data with anyone.

Donations

If you choose to make a voluntary donation, payment is processed entirely by Stripe. Stripe collects the information needed to process the payment (such as your card details and email address) under Stripe's own privacy policy. We never see or store your card details. If you optionally provide your name or alias for the Hall of Fame at checkout, that name is displayed publicly on the support page — leaving it blank keeps you private. You can request removal of your listed name at any time by emailing us.

Cookies

The app itself does not require cookies to function. Analytics services may use cookies or similar identifiers for anonymized measurement.
