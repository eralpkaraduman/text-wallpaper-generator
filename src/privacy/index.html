Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

TextWallpaper.com has no accounts, no sign-ups, and collects no personal data from you. Your wallpapers are generated entirely inside your browser. The text you type never leaves your device.

What happens in your browser

Everything. The text you enter, the colors you pick, and the images you create are processed and rendered locally on your device. No wallpaper content, text, or images get sent to or stored on any server.

Analytics

The site uses anonymized usage analytics so I can see which features get used, never who is using them. That means aggregate page views and feature events, like how often the download button gets clicked. I don't build profiles of individual visitors, and I don't sell or share analytics data with anyone.

Donations

If you choose to donate, payment is handled entirely by Stripe. Stripe collects what it needs to process the payment, like your card details and email address, under Stripe's own privacy policy. I never see or store your card details. If you type your name or alias into the Hall of Fame field at checkout, that name goes on the public support page. Leave it blank and nothing about you is shown anywhere. Want your name taken down later? Email me and it's gone.

Cookies

The app itself does not require cookies to function. Analytics services may use cookies or similar identifiers for anonymized measurement.
