Support This Project

Text Wallpaper is a free, private app made by one person. Donations pay the server bills and keep the app free for everyone. No pressure. The app works the same either way.

Why donations?

There is no account, no tracking, and nothing sold or shared. I only keep anonymous usage stats: which features get used, never who used them. There are no ads and no paid tier. Every feature is free for everyone. Ads or subscriptions would make the app worse, so I keep them out.

This is a one-person project. I build it in my free time, next to a day job and a family. The servers cost real money every month, and I pay for them myself. Donations keep the app online and free.

The app is also open source on GitHub. Anyone can read the code and check that these promises are true.

Got an idea for a feature?

The checkout has an optional “feature request” field. Write your idea there. I read every one, and donor ideas come first when I pick what to build next.
