Support This Project

Text Wallpaper is built to be private and simple, and staying free is part of how it stays that way.

There's no account to create, nothing tracked about who you are, and nothing sold or shared — I keep only anonymized usage data, the kind that tells me which features get used, never who's using them. There are no ads, and there's no paid “pro” tier either — every feature works the same for everyone, with nothing held back behind a paywall. Adding accounts, subscriptions, or tracking would make the app worse to use just to make it profitable, and that trade isn't one I want to make.

This is a one-person project. I build and maintain it myself, around a day job and a family, in whatever time I can find. It doesn't run on a company budget — hosting and infrastructure still cost real money every month, and it's just me behind all of it.

If you'd like to help keep it running and growing, a donation goes straight toward covering those costs and funding whatever gets built next. There's no obligation at all — the app stays exactly as free and private either way.
