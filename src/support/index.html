Support This Project

Text Wallpaper is a free, private app made by one person. A donation helps pay the hosting bills and funds whatever gets built next. No obligation. The app stays just as free either way.

Donate via Stripe ❤

Why donations?

There's no account to create, nothing tracked about who you are, and nothing sold or shared. I keep only anonymized usage data: which features get used, never who's using them. There are no ads and no paid “pro” tier. Every feature works the same for everyone. Adding accounts, subscriptions, or tracking would make the app worse just to make it profitable, and I don't want to make that trade.

This is a one-person project. I build and maintain it myself, around a day job and a family, in whatever time I can find. There's no company budget behind it. Hosting still costs real money every month, and it's just me paying for it.

Got an idea for a feature?

The checkout has an optional “feature request” field. Leave a note with your donation. I read every one, and donor requests come first when I decide what to build next.
