Desktop Wallpaper Maker — Custom Text Wallpapers for PC & Mac

Turn any text into a clean desktop background — a reminder that stays in view all day, a quote, your name, or a minimal one-word wallpaper. This free desktop wallpaper creator runs in your browser: type your text, pick colors, choose a resolution, download.

Because the wallpaper is generated (not stretched from a photo), it comes out pixel-perfect at any size — Full HD, QHD, or a crisp 4K text wallpaper for a large monitor. It works the same as a laptop wallpaper maker too: just use your laptop's screen size.

Common Desktop & Laptop Resolutions

  • 1920×1080 (Full HD) — most laptops and standard monitors
  • 2560×1440 (QHD) — 27-inch monitors and higher-end laptops
  • 3840×2160 (4K UHD) — 4K monitors and TVs used as displays
  • 3440×1440 / 2560×1080 — ultrawide monitors
  • Mac displays — e.g. 2560×1664 (MacBook Air 13″), 3024×1964 (MacBook Pro 14″), 5120×2880 (Studio Display)

Not sure what yours is? On Windows check Settings → System → Display; on a Mac, System Settings → Displays. Enter that width and height in the maker and your HD or 4K wallpaper will match your screen exactly.

How to Set Your New Wallpaper

Windows 10 / 11

  1. Create and download your wallpaper from TextWallpaper.com.
  2. Right-click the downloaded image and choose Set as desktop background — or right-click the desktop, choose Personalize → Background, and browse to the file.

macOS

  1. Download your wallpaper (it lands in your Downloads folder).
  2. Open System Settings → Wallpaper, click Add Photo / Add Folder, and pick the file. Or right-click the image in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture.

What People Use Text Wallpapers For

  • A standing reminder — a deadline, a goal, or this week's priority, visible every time you close a window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free?

Yes — free, no watermark, no sign-up.

Can I make a 4K wallpaper?

Yes. Enter 3840×2160 (or any custom size, including 5K and ultrawide). Since the image is rendered from text, it's sharp at every resolution — no upscaled, blurry photos.

What resolution should I pick?

Your monitor's native resolution — see the list above, or check your display settings.

Windows and Mac both?

Yes. The maker runs in any browser, and the downloaded image works as a background on Windows, macOS, Linux, or ChromeOS.
